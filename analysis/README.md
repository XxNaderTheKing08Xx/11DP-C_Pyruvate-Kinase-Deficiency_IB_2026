# Analysis notes

This folder contains the three analyses behind the report on pyruvate kinase deficiency caused by *PKLR* c.1456C>T, p.(Arg486Trp). Each subfolder has its own `README.md` (aim, data sources with accessions, versions, and access dates, steps and parameters, results, conclusions, reproduction, file index), its scripts, its result tables, and the raw inputs needed to repeat it. All database records were accessed on 22 October 2026. Scripts run from inside their subfolder (for example `cd analysis/sequence && python scripts/01_characterize.py`); the Python packages are listed in each `requirements.txt`. The final figures are in the repository folder `figures/`. `MANIFEST.md` lists every file with its size.

**[`sequence/`](sequence/README.md)** — We translated and compared the supplied wild-type and mutant *PKLR* sequences, matched them to the R-type reference records (NM_000298.6, NP_000289.1, UniProt P30613), and named the single difference NM_000298.6:c.1456C>T, NP_000289.1:p.(Arg486Trp). We selected the reviewed PKLR orthologues of rat (P12928), mouse (P53657), and dog (Q29536), confirmed orthology with Ensembl Compara and by identity to PKLR versus the paralogue PKM, and compared them by pairwise alignment (91.1%, 91.6%, and 92.5% protein identity to human) and by multiple alignment with Clustal Omega 1.2.4. At the position of human Arg486, rat has Gln and mouse and dog have Arg; of 170 jawed-vertebrate PKLR sequences with a resolved residue, 156 have Arg and 13 have Gln (the remaining one, a Tyr, is in a gene model that is probably misassembled), and Trp was not observed in any of the 190 Ensembl Compara homologues. Figure 1 and Table 2 come from this analysis.

**[`structure/`](structure/README.md)** — We analysed the crystal structures of human R-type pyruvate kinase, wild type (PDB 2VGB) and R486W (PDB 2VGI), and of rat L-type pyruvate kinase (PDB 6ECH, 6ECK). Arg486 lies in the C domain at the contact between the A and C domains of one subunit, is largely buried, hydrogen-bonds the main-chain carbonyl of Leu362, and lies about 12 Å from the allosteric activator fructose 1,6-bisphosphate and about 24 Å from the active site. The R486W structure superposes on the wild type with Cα RMSD 0.40–0.59 Å per subunit, and the differences around residue 486 are within the range found elsewhere in the two models. In rat, Gln455 (= R-type 486) does not form the interdomain hydrogen bond. Figures 2 and 3 and the table of structural measurements come from this analysis.

**[`evidence/`](evidence/README.md)** — We collected the variant-level evidence for c.1456C>T (ClinVar VCV000001513, Pathogenic/Likely pathogenic, 33 submitters; gnomAD v4.1 allele frequency 0.295% with eight homozygotes; dbSNP rs116100695; UniProt VAR_004066), reviewed the literature on its frequency among patients, genotype–phenotype relations, functional properties (Valentini *et al.* 2002: reduced catalytic efficiency with preserved thermostability and allosteric regulation), and response to mitapivat, and traced the chain from the variant to the patient's phenotype, diagnosis, and treatment. Table 1 and the reference list (`references_used.md`) come from this analysis.
