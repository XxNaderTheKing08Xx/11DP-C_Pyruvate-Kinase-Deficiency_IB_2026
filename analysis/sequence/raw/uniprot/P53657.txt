CC   -!- FUNCTION: Pyruvate kinase that catalyzes the conversion of
CC       phosphoenolpyruvate to pyruvate with the synthesis of ATP, and which
CC       plays a key role in glycolysis. Also produces the side product 2-
CC       phospholactate which can inhibit fructose-2,6-bisphosphate production.
CC       2-phospholactate can be dephosphorylated by PGP which prevents the
CC       inhibition of fructose-2,6-bisphosphate production and allows
CC       glycolysis to occur. {ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P30613}.
